A leather sphere, a willow blade,
A patch of green where dreams are made.
Beneath the wide and shifting sky,
The fielders wait, the shadows fly.

The bowler runs, a rhythmic stride,
With fire and fury deep inside.
A flick of wrist, the ball takes flight,
A streak of red in brilliant light.

The batsman stands, a watchful guard,
Eyes locked upon the turf so hard.
A crack of willow, crisp and clean,
The finest sound you’ve ever seen.

The ball goes racing to the rope,
A sudden burst of joy and hope.
Or maybe, with a sudden spin,
The bails fly up, the fielding win.

From Test match days of quiet grace,
To short formats' frantic pace,
It’s more than just a simple game,
It’s passion, country, heart, and fame.

For when the final over’s done,
Beneath the setting, golden sun,
Opponents shake each other's hand—
The finest spirit in the land.
